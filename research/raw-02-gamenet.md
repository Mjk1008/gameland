# RAW — Iran Gamenet (گیم‌نت) Landscape (research agent 2)

> Preserved for traceability. Synthesized into [`../docs/02-market-data.md`](../docs/02-market-data.md). USD ≈ 700–750k Toman (mid-2025, volatile).

## 1. Count & trend
- **302 licensed gamenets (بازیگاه)** — IRCG official registry portal.ircg.ir/home/gnet_public [H, undercount].
- **~12,709 cafenet+gamenet** — Bankyab commercial DB [M].
- **Tehran ~2,000 total, ~200 licensed (~90% unlicensed)** [L ratio, H phenomenon].
- Trend: old کافی‌نت (internet-only) declining since ~2015; premium گیم‌سنتر (gaming PCs + console rooms) rising 2022–25 [M]. No public YoY open/close data.

## 2. Business model & pricing (1403–04 / 2024–25)
| Service | Toman/hr | Conf |
|---|---|---|
| Standard PC | 30–50k | H |
| VIP PC | 50–80k | H |
| PS5 standard | 90–100k | H |
| PS5 VIP | 120–150k | H |
| VR (PSVR2) | 120–150k | M |
| Premium PS5 (Takaf) | up to 200k | H |
- Hourly rental model (no subscription). Add-ons: café/buffet, tournaments, VR. 3–5× price rise in 2–3 yrs (Toman depreciation).
- PC vs console: both common; large venues PC-heavy (Volcano 59 PC+2 PS5), provincial console-heavy (Game Spot Rasht 6 PC+35 PS5). PES/FIFA ~80% of console time.
- Scale: small 6–8 systems → premium 38–60 PCs + console room.
- Revenue (advisory models, LOW conf): small ~12–15M T/mo; mid 25–50M; modeled 150M. Startup: 100–300M T equipment; 200M–1.6B total. Rent Tehran 50m² 20–50M T/mo. Payback 1.5–3 yrs [L].

## 3. Licensing (dual authority)
- **IRCG (بنیاد ملی بازی‌های رایانه‌ای)** under Min. Culture — issues "بازیگاه" license (portal.ircg.ir) [H].
- **اتحادیه/اتاق اصناف (صنف رایانه)** — commercial جواز کسب [H].
- IRCG reqs: citizen, age ≥20, military done (M), clean record, competency exam, **≥25m²**, visible interior, fire safety, police inspection, **ESRA-rated games only**, age separation, **cannot combine with café/internet-café**, **separate permit per tournament**, 1-yr renewable, ≤7 days processing.

## 4. Role in esports / community
- Gamenets = primary **physical esports infra**; grassroots LAN tournaments most common [H].
- Active organizers: **ICG (Iran Cyber Games)** event.icg.ir · **Cube** (est. 2008; IGL 2017 7,000+ participants, final on TV Channel 3; ESWC FIFA partner) · **Oxin Game** (CS2 S1, 400M Toman prize) · **Iran Tournament** (E-Sports Summer) · **Phonix Game** · **Iran Game Tour** (weekly online+in-person).
- Infinity Gamenet (Tehran D3) markets as "pro esports training facility."
- Constraint: Steam/PSN/Xbox Live blocked; VPN/DNS universal among serious gamers but crackdowns disable.

## 5. Geography
- Tehran highest (all 22 districts; premium in D2/3/5). Confirmed active: Mashhad, Isfahan, Tabriz (15+), Karaj, Rasht, Bandar Abbas, Shiraz, + more. Across all 31 provinces. No province % public.

## 6. Directories / associations / chains
- Directories: IRCG registry (302), **Balad.ir** (best live dir: addresses/phone/reviews), Behtarino/kikhobe/iranitop, Bankyab (~12,709 paid).
- **No independent gamenet trade association** [data gap H].
- **Only multi-branch chain = Sicily (2 Tehran branches).** No formal franchise network exists [M].

## 7. Pains
- **Currency:** Rial −37% vs USD in 2024 alone; hardware priced in USD → constant repricing.
- **Hardware/sanctions:** OFAC June 2024 bans export of >1 WeightedTeraFLOP compute to Iran; GPUs/PS5 via gray market (Turkey/UAE) 2–4× retail; NVIDIA refuses Iran support.
- **Electricity:** ~14,000 MW summer deficit; rolling blackouts; Dec 2024 closures. 30–60 PCs × 12h/day = energy-intensive; blackout = acute threat.
- **Internet:** 40% <15 Mbps; 62% ping 100–150ms; only 5% <100ms. Steam/PSN/Xbox/Fortnite/Rockstar blocked; VPN universal. Crackdowns (e.g. Jan 2026 outage) halt revenue 2–3 months to recover. **IRCG offering dedicated intl internet to licensed gamenets (static IP), not fully rolled out** [M].
- **Regulatory:** 1-yr renewals, per-event tournament permit, ESRA approval delays, no café combo.
- **Competition:** mobile dominates (94.5% smartphone primary).

## 8. Korea PC-bang reference
- Peak ~25,000 (2009–11) → ~9,265 (2021) → **~8,152 (Apr 2023)**; −62% [H]. Rev 2022 ~$1.6B. Rate $0.75–1.12/hr. Avg session 169 min; regulars 7.2 visits/mo. Viable at national-network scale but vulnerable to mobile/home shift. F&B now key supplement.
