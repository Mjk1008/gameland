# RAW — Iran Gaming & Esports Market (research agent 1)

> Raw research output, preserved for traceability. Synthesized into [`../docs/02-market-data.md`](../docs/02-market-data.md). Confidence tags [H/M/L] per claim.

## 1. Total gamers & growth
- **34M gamers (2022)** — IRCG/DIREC national survey [M]. Minister Zarepour cited **30M** (Dec 2022) [H].
- Trajectory (IRCG): 23M (2015) → 28M (2018) → 32M (2019/20) → ~34M (2022) [M].
- Penetration ~**34–40%** of population [M].
- Statista "14.7M by 2027" = narrower *monetized* users metric, not all players.

## 3. Platform split
- **IRCG 2022 (usage, multi-select):** mobile **96%**, console **88%**, PC **59%**, laptop 48%, tablet 14%, handheld 13%; multi-platform 33% [M].
- 2023 NCFG: smartphones 94.5% of mobile gamers.
- Revenue share: mobile ~65% [M].
- **Mobile dominance is economic** (console/PC costly via gray market), not preference.

## 4. Demographics
- Age: avg **25** (2022 IRCG) / **29** (2023 NCFG); rising from ~19 (2018) [M].
- Gender: **41% female / 59% male** (2022), up from 35% (2018) [M].
- Geo: most gamers **Alborz** (near Tehran); fewest Qom. No city % breakdown public [M]. FLAG.

## 5. Popular games
- PC (DIREC 2018): GTA, PES, Counter-Strike, Call of Duty, FIFA [H, dated].
- Mobile: Clash of Clans (later banned), Subway Surfers, Mafia City, etc.; 2025 downloads skew casual/sim.
- Esports-active titles (IEA): **CS2, eFootball, Mobile Legends, Dota 2, VALORANT** [H existence].
- FLAG: no 2023–24 cross-platform popularity ranking public.

## 6. Esports
- Governance: **Iran Electronic Sports Association (IESA)** — Min. of Sport recognized; IESF/GEF/AESF member; under NOCIRI [H].
- Earnings (esportsearnings): all-time **$1.07M**, 171 players, 352 tournaments, world #80. **Chess = 53%** (Firouzja $606K, competes for France → inflates "Iran"). Real Iran-rep leader = Dota 2 ($89K).
- 2024 events: IESF CS2 Iranian qualifier (15 teams, champ FG Metarix); Dota2 CyberEast 3rd ($2,335); women's GEG ME qualifier won; ~9 VALORANT domestic tournaments.
- **Viewership tiny**: Iran-organized CS2 event peak **248 viewers** [H]. Domestic streams on Aparat/Telegram untracked internationally. FLAG.
- Sponsors: none identified publicly. Zoomit (40M+ views/mo), Digiato = big tech media.

## 7. Console pricing & infra (gray market)
- **No official Sony/Xbox distributor**; imported via UAE. PS5 ~54M T disc (~$1,286), digital ~$1,024; **~2.6× MSRP** [H]. ~10–12 months avg wages to afford a PS5 [M].
- PC gamers ~7.2M (2018) [H, dated]. Windows ~98.5%.
- **Internet: 4.0–4.8 Mbps avg, 136–146 ms latency**, bottom-50 globally [H]. **~64% use VPN** ($5–6/mo) [M]. Feb 2024 VPN crackdown ordered [H].
- **Sanctions block AWS/Azure/GCP, HTTP3/QUIC**; no direct Steam/PSN/Battle.net; can't publish to App Store/Google Play → **local stores Cafebazaar (40M+), Myket, Plazza, Iranapps** [H].
