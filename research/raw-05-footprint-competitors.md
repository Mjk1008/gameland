## Key takeaways
1. **Marcoverse = sharpest direct competitor** (social network + store + competitions + offline prelims in 6 cities) — and it's the Marcocial sister product. Position against it explicitly.
2. **OxinGame = dominant brand/venue player.** 
3. Market is **fragmented by game** (PUBG: iran-esport/bazichi; CS: OxinGame/BATTLEGAME; FIFA: Marcoverse/IRCG). **No one owns cross-game persistent identity + ranking + gamenet network** → confirms whitespace.
4. Gameland starts **digitally near-zero** despite offline community → GTM must lean on offline community + gamenet partnerships, not existing online reach.
