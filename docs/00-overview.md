# 00 — Overview

## Founder direction (2026-06-12 update)
- **Mission:** bring gamers closer to gaming, *by any means.* Gameland is **one of several paths** toward this (likely the theme across all 3 businesses).
- **Name:** **Gameland** — confirmed (keep despite unrelated "Gameland" name clashes).
- **Market:** Iran (for now).
- **Current focus (this build):** ① persistent **ranking** · ② the **data we already have** + collecting more (**Gamer Bank**) · ③ **proper competition execution** · ④ **notifications / info dissemination.** Gamenet network, store, and AI bot = **later ideas** (deferred — gamenet still the strategic moat).
- **Competitive view:** no real competitor builds the *integrated* vision. Even Marcocial/Marcoverse cover only one slice (competition hosting).
- **Legal:** competitions are already run widely (incl. IRCG itself); **permits come later — not a build-blocker.** Design is kept legally-safe by default (sponsor-funded prizes, non-convertible coins) so permits stay attainable. Risk scales with cash-prize size — see [11-risks](11-risks.md).

## Vision (working)
A single platform that turns Gameland's offline-first gaming community into a durable digital network: where every player has a **professional identity**, every competition runs and communicates itself, a **national ranking** accumulates over time, and gaming-cafés (گیم‌نت) become connected nodes of the ecosystem.

## Who Gameland is today
- Brand: **Gameland / گیم‌لند**, Instagram **@Gamelandteam**.
- Several years running gaming competitions in Iran; large community.
- ~**70% of competitions are offline / in-person.**
- Today's coordination tools: Instagram + WhatsApp (manual, repetitive Q&A) + ad-hoc use of [marcocial.com](https://marcocial.com/p/gameland).

## The 4 pillars (from the brief)

### 1. Gamer Bank (بانک گیمرها)
A complete, professional profile per player: identity, contact channel, city, which disciplines/games they compete in, play-style (e.g. aggressive), nickname, and a **public honors page** ("16× Iran champion in FIFA…"). Top players get featured (photos, stats); everyone accrues a record.

### 2. Tournament platform (مثل مارکوشیال، بهتر)
- Annual competition **calendar**.
- **Pro notification system** per competition — schedule, format, rules, "everything people keep asking on WhatsApp."
- **Per-player roadmap** — each player sees their own path through the bracket: where they are, who they face, how many wins to advance.
- **Multi-account detection** — today they cannot detect multi-accounting at all.
- **Ticket / chance model** (their exact mechanic):
  - Knockout format. Players can buy **1–6 entry tickets ("chances")** before a competition.
  - Each competition has **6 preliminary brackets**; the best from each advance.
  - Example: 2 tickets → play bracket 1 (lose game 4, out) → play bracket 2 (win 6, advance). 
  - Each player can carry up to **3 seeds** into the final.
  - **Final = 128 players.**

### 3. Network & commerce
- **Gamenet network** — register گیم‌نت/gaming-cafés; profile per café (location, resources, #gamers); host competitions for/at them; talent-scouting (استعدادیابی) and call-outs (فراخوان). Goal: connect gamers to cafés & gaming services nationwide.
- **Store / marketplace** — supply & demand for what gamers want: games, packages, controllers (دسته), etc.
- **AI support bot** — an assistant that "knows everything" and guides users.

### 4. Persistent ranking (رنکینگ)
- **Offline-first** (online/ultimate ranking deferred ~1 year due to connectivity).
- Points per placement per event, weighted by event tier. Examples:
  - Major: champion **1000**, 2nd **800**, 3rd **500**.
  - All-Star (smaller seasonal): 1st **500**, 2nd **300**, 3rd **150**, top-32 **30**.
  - Event tier can scale points (e.g. a technically Gameland-run event = 800-pt).
- Leaderboard: top ~10 featured with photos; everyone else listed with name + #games.
- Individual pages (e.g. "Nima Sadeghi") with honors, titles, character/play-style.

## Key benchmark insight (see [01](01-benchmark.md))
Gameland's real structural twin is **Smoothcomp** (combat-sports: offline-first, per-federation persistent points ranking, club network, per-athlete economics) — **not** the online esports platforms (FACEIT, Start.gg). The **gaming-café network** is whitespace that even Smoothcomp lacks.
