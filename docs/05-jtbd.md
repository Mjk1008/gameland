# 05 — Jobs To Be Done

**Stage:** Idea · **Framework:** JTBD · **Status:** ✅ v1 (data-grounded).

## Primary jobs

**J1 — Player: "Know exactly where I stand and what happens next."**
- *Functional:* see my schedule, format, rules, my bracket path, who I face, wins-to-advance.
- *Emotional:* stop the anxiety of pestering WhatsApp; feel in control.
- *Evidence:* dark-social coordination chaos confirmed ([02 §5](02-market-data.md)); the brief's "everything people keep asking on WhatsApp."

**J2 — Player: "Make my wins count beyond the day."**
- *Functional:* a permanent profile + honors + national ranking.
- *Social:* recognition as a real competitor ("16× champion").
- *Evidence:* no Iranian platform offers persistent cross-game identity ([01](01-benchmark.md)/[02](02-market-data.md)); Liquipedia proves demand for honors pages (pros only).

**J3 — Organizer: "Run a multi-bracket competition and announce it once."**
- *Functional:* brackets, entries, notifications, results — without 200 repeat questions.
- *Emotional:* look professional; reduce ops load.
- *Constraint:* must be **legally clean** (sponsor prizes, skill framing) — [11](11-risks.md).

**J4 — Gamenet owner: "Fill seats and get discovered."**
- *Functional:* a profile, hosting tools, visibility to players/organizers, talent-scouting.
- *Economic:* more utilization amid fragile economics ([02 §2](02-market-data.md)).
- *Evidence:* no directory/network/association exists.

**J5 — Community/fan: "See who's actually the best."**
- *Functional:* trusted national leaderboard + player pages to follow.

## Anti-jobs / what NOT to build into
- Players are **not** hiring Gameland to gamble — keep entry a **skill service**, prizes sponsor-funded ([11](11-risks.md) R1).
- Not hiring it to replace their Telegram/WhatsApp overnight — **integrate** first ([11](11-risks.md) R7).

## Job priority (for [07](07-opportunity-tree.md))
J1 + J2 (player control + identity/ranking) are the **wedge value**; J4 (gamenet) is the **moat**; J3 (organizer tools) **scales supply**; J5 follows.
