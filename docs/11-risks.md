# 11 — Risk Register

**Stage:** Plan · **Status:** ⏳ pending Data

Top risks to document & rate (likelihood × impact) + mitigation:
- **⚠️ Legal — gambling/قمار:** paid "chances/tickets" to win prizes may be read as betting. Needs the skill-competition framing + possibly removing cash-prize linkage / permit. **Highest-priority risk.**
- **Payments:** sanctions, gateway approval (eNamad), IAP limits → monetization friction.
- **Filtering / distribution:** platform/app reachability, reliance on Cafe Bazaar/Myket, VPN dependence.
- **Offline integrity:** multi-account detection without kernel anti-cheat (offline = identity-at-registration).
- **Adoption:** moving organizers/players off Instagram+WhatsApp habit.
- **Connectivity:** poor internet → offline-first architecture required.
- **Hardware/economy:** gamenet viability under import costs & inflation.
