# 04 — Positioning

**Stage:** Idea · **Framework:** Geoffrey Moore positioning · **Status:** ✅ v1 (data-grounded).

## Statement
> **For** Iran's competitive gamers and grassroots tournament organizers — who compete mostly **offline** and coordinate chaotically across Instagram/Telegram/WhatsApp,
> **Gameland is** an offline-first gaming **competition + identity + network** platform,
> **that** gives every player a persistent professional profile and a **national ranking**, runs and announces competitions end-to-end (with sponsor-funded prizes), and connects players to a network of **gaming-cafés (گیم‌نت)**,
> **unlike** **Marcoverse** (online-first social/competitions), **OxinGame** (single-brand venues), or **Marcocial** (generic event hosting with thin identity),
> **because** it is built around the Iranian reality — offline-first, mobile, domestically-hosted, sponsor-prize (legally clean), and cross-game — applying the proven **Smoothcomp** federation model to gaming, plus the **gamenet network no competitor owns.**

## Three pillars of differentiation (data-backed)
1. **Persistent cross-game identity + national ranking** — unclaimed in Iran ([02 §5](02-market-data.md)); market is siloed by single game.
2. **Gamenet network** — zero aggregator exists (only Sicily's 2 branches; no association) ([02 §2](02-market-data.md)). The wedge.
3. **Legally-clean, offline-first competition engine** — sponsor-funded prizes + coin wallet sidestep the gambling line ([11](11-risks.md)) that an online stake-to-prize competitor would trip.

## Category
Not "esports tournament software" (Toornament/Start.gg) and not "online competition social network" (Marcoverse). Category = **"the home of competitive gaming in Iran"** — identity + ranking + competitions + venues, offline-first.

## Proof obligations (must earn)
- A player honors page people actually want (Liquipedia-grade, but self-serve & amateur).
- A ranking the community trusts as "who's actually best."
- Gamenets that measurably gain traffic by joining.
