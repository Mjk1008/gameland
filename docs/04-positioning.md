# 04 — Positioning

**Stage:** Idea · **Framework:** Geoffrey Moore positioning statement · **Status:** ⏳ pending Data

Working draft (to refine with data):
> **For** Iranian competitive gamers and grassroots tournament organizers
> **who** run and play mostly-offline competitions coordinated chaotically over Instagram/WhatsApp,
> **Gameland is** a gaming tournament + community platform
> **that** gives every player a persistent professional identity & national ranking, runs/announces competitions end-to-end, and connects them to a network of gaming-cafés,
> **unlike** Marcocial (event hosting, thin identity) or FACEIT/Start.gg (online-only, no offline ranking, no café network)
> **because** it is built offline-first around the Iranian gaming reality — the Smoothcomp model applied to gaming.
