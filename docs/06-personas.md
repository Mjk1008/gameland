# 06 — Proto-Personas

**Stage:** Idea · **Framework:** proto-persona · **Status:** ⏳ pending Data

Personas to define (draft set):
1. **The Competitor** — young player, multiple events, wants ranking & honors.
2. **The First-Timer** — buys extra "chances", needs the roadmap & rules clarity.
3. **The Organizer** — runs offline brackets, drowning in WhatsApp coordination.
4. **The Gamenet Owner** — wants foot traffic, hosting, visibility.
5. **The Fan/Spectator** — follows top players & rankings.

> Each: demographics, behaviors, goals, frustrations, current workaround.
