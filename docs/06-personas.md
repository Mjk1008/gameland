# 06 — Proto-Personas

**Stage:** Idea · **Framework:** proto-persona · **Status:** ✅ v1 (data-grounded; avg gamer age 25–29, ~41% female, mobile-first, gamenet-going).

## P1 — "Reza", the Competitor (core)
- 19–28, male, Tehran/Karaj; plays eFootball/EA FC + CS2; competes offline at gamenets + Gameland events.
- **Goals:** climb the national ranking, build a name, win sponsor prizes.
- **Frustrations:** wins vanish; no record; WhatsApp chaos; smurfs ruin fairness.
- **Wants:** honors page, ranking, clear bracket roadmap.

## P2 — "Sara", the Rising Player (growth segment)
- 17–24, female (reflecting the rising ~41% female share); mobile-first; newer to organized play.
- **Goals:** compete safely, improve, belong to a community.
- **Frustrations:** intimidating/opaque events; gets knocked out in one loss as a first-timer.
- **Wants:** extra attempts (the "chances" mechanic — **as a skill service, not a bet**), clear rules, welcoming community.

## P3 — "Amir", the Organizer
- 25–35; runs recurring offline tournaments; today juggles Instagram + Telegram bot + WhatsApp + Aparat.
- **Goals:** run pro events, grow audience, monetize via sponsors.
- **Frustrations:** repetitive Q&A, manual brackets, no notifications, no legal clarity on prizes.
- **Wants:** end-to-end tooling + a legitimacy umbrella (IRCG/IESA-aligned).

## P4 — "Hossein", the Gamenet Owner
- 30–45; owns a 10–40 station گیم‌نت; squeezed by rent/hardware/electricity.
- **Goals:** more foot traffic, host events, get discovered, scout talent.
- **Frustrations:** invisible online; no network; licensing hassle; no tools.
- **Wants:** a profile, booking/host tools, a place in a real network.

## P5 — "Nima", the Fan/Spectator
- 15–30; follows top players & events on Aparat/Instagram.
- **Goals:** follow heroes, see rankings, watch finals.
- **Wants:** player pages, leaderboards, event calendar.

## Note
Personas are provisional (built on aggregate market data + the brief, not yet on Gameland's own interviews). **Validate via discovery interviews** with Gameland's existing offline community before locking the PRD.
